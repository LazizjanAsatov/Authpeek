AuthPeek is ready

Your 2-step codes now live in your browser, and they fill themselves in when a site asks. Three short steps and you're set.

  1. 1

    Create your password

    Click the AuthPeek icon in the toolbar and choose a password.

    Don't see the icon? Click the puzzle piece, then the pin next to AuthPeek so it stays in view.

    Already use AuthPeek on another computer? Don't make a new password. Wait a minute for sync, then unlock with the one you have.

    Extensions AuthPeek
  2. 2

    Add your accounts

    Open AuthPeek, click +, and scan the QR code a site shows when you turn on 2-step verification.

    Moving from Google Authenticator™? Choose Transfer accounts there and scan its QR code to bring everything over at once.

    A284 913 B570 266 C039 418
  3. 3

    Sign in as usual

    The first time a site asks for a code, open AuthPeek and choose Fill on the right account.

    AuthPeek links the account to that site. From then on, the code fills itself.

Good to know

  • Your codes are encrypted with your password before they sync, so only you can read them.

  • Nobody can reset your password, so make a backup in Settings and keep it somewhere safe.

  • Alt+Shift+O fills the code on the page you're on.

  • You can share a code for a few minutes without sharing the account.
