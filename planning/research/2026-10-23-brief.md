# AuthPeek research brief — 2026-10-23

## 1. Why you can't rate, why friends' reviews don't show

**Lead point:** Google's Spam FAQ answers "Can I review or rate my own extension?" with "No," and extends that to "ratings and reviews coming from other developers or users that are affiliated with the publisher." The Program Policies say manipulating "your extension's reviews or ratings" means "you will be banned from the Chrome Web Store." Your own rating and your friends' reviews are the exact case the policy targets, so stop soliciting them. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/spam-faq, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies)

**Verified (verified:true) facts**

- The user-facing Store ToS (updated 2025-01-27) §3.11 bans reviews "if you own or work for the business that created it," reviews "on behalf of others," and reviews "for the same content from multiple accounts." (https://ssl.gstatic.com/chrome/webstore/intl/en/gallery_tos.html)
- The reviews page filters to the store UI language by default; the "Language" menu has exactly two options, the current language (pre-selected) and "All languages." Confirmed live 2026-10-23 on store HTML; the `?hl=<locale>` trick still changes the server-rendered set. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/9lc7Prf9vLk, https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/btools/codgofkgobbmgglciccjabipdlgefnch/reviews?hl=en)
- Reviews can be "held for moderation or … deleted. Unfortunately, we don't currently have a way to get more details there" (Oliver Dunk, Chrome DevRel, 2025-12-03). No timeframe is published; DevRel: "wait a couple of days before becoming concerned" (2024-11-22). A store-wide outage ran ~Nov 6–19, 2024. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/WApITOktFSA, https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/08mN-8UZvoY)
- Current incident: Google's Patrick Kettner, 2026-10-22: "There has been a delay in some analytics and review data since October 16." A developer reported five review emails Oct 20–22 with the public count frozen since Oct 17. This is the most likely reason reviews posted this week are invisible. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/1ZXoQy_eKBg)
- Header rating "calculated periodically, and so they can lag" (Dunk, 2025-11-27). Since Aug 20, 2026 the rating "will focus on more recent reviews"; a developer reports the dashboard histogram still looks all-time. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/WApITOktFSA, https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-review-updates-2026, https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/maQqE5X60Lg)
- Developers reply to reviews (with a "Developer" badge) but "cannot remove user comments"; the dashboard Rating tab sits under Analytics; email alerts need "Item review completed" enabled in Account settings > Notifications. One developer saw a held review missing from the dashboard too. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/support-users, https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-analytics-revamp)
- No policy text forbids a plain, non-incentivized request to genuine users; Google's own doc shows how to hand users a direct link by appending `/reviews` to the item URL. Incentives are banned. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/support-users, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/spam-and-abuse)
- Other rules: no "unattributed or anonymous user testimonials" in the description; no metadata misrepresenting status (e.g. "Editor's Choice"); one appeal per violation; default two extension slots per publisher. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies, https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-review-updates-2026)

**Not confirmed** (verified:false in the research)

- "Only installers can review" — Google's wording is that installed users "can" review; the mechanism, and whether the UI blocks the publisher's own account, is undocumented. (https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/12225786?hl=en)
- "Main page dropped review bodies in Jan 2025" — reports start Dec 2024; Google confirmed Jan 17, 2025; the "0 reviews" header was never confirmed fixed. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/WApITOktFSA)
- "Listing still unpublished" — your site says "in review," but the verifier noted that friends writing reviews at all implies a live item page; the "in review" line may be stale or refer to a version update. (https://lazizjanasatov.github.io/Authpeek/, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/update)
- "No age restriction" — refuted: ToS §1.4 requires users to be 13+, 13–17 with parental permission. Minimum-ratings threshold and region partitioning remain undocumented. (https://ssl.gstatic.com/chrome/webstore/intl/en/gallery_tos.html)

**Checklist**

1. Developer Dashboard: confirm item status is Published (statuses: Published, Pending, Rejected, Taken Down); copy the 32-char ID. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/check-review)
2. Open `https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/<id>/reviews`, set Sort by = Recent, Language = All languages. Also try `?hl=ru` / `?hl=uz`.
3. Dashboard > Analytics > Ratings for the count; Account settings > Notifications > enable "Item review completed."
4. Wait a few days given the Oct 16 data delay; then post the extension ID and forward review emails in the chromium-extensions thread. (https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/1ZXoQy_eKBg)
5. Tell friends: nothing more. Their reviews are "affiliated" under policy. If any contain links or emails, they violate review rules and are a plausible removal cause. (https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/1050673?hl=en)
6. Do not: rate from any account, offer incentives, use multiple accounts, put testimonials in the description, or claim ratings/featured status you don't have.
7. Allowed: a neutral "Rate AuthPeek" link to `/reviews` in the options page or site, no reward attached.

(https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/12225786?hl=en, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/discovery, https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-review-updates-2026)

(https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/best-practices, https://extscope.org/extension/bhghoamapcdpbohphigoooaddinpkbai)

(https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/cws-dashboard-listing#displaying-your-verified-publisher-status, https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080)
6. **Screenshots:** five at 1280×800, full-bleed, actual current UX, little text; 440×280 small tile shows in search results. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/cws-dashboard-listing)
7. **Localization:** add `_locales/` with `default_locale`, then per-locale description/screenshots in the dashboard. Google promises no ranking boost, only that users "search for extensions in their own language." Never ship per-language copies (repetitive-content violation; two-slot limit). (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/reference/api/i18n, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/spam-faq)
8. **Operational:** indexing takes hours; check Distribution tab regions; title/summary live in the manifest, so changes need a version upload. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/discovery, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/prepare)

## 5. Share feature

**Lead point:** Ente Auth is the only shipped authenticator that shares codes without the secret: pre-computed codes for 2/5/10 min, encrypted with a random 256-bit key, key only in the URL fragment, decrypted by a static page. AuthPeek can copy it 1:1 with GitHub Pages as the viewer. (https://ente.com/help/auth/features, https://github.com/ente-io/ente/blob/main/web/apps/auth/src/pages/share.tsx)

**A. Share codes (recommended default).** Compute `ceil(minutes*60/period)` codes, JSON `{startTime, step, codes}`, AES-GCM with a random key, and put ciphertext *and* key in the fragment: `https://lazizjanasatov.github.io/Authpeek/share#<payload>`. Fragments are never sent to the server (RFC 3986 §3.5), so GitHub receives nothing; the same link pasted into the popup on another computer parses `location.hash`. Viewer shows current + next code, countdown, "expired" and "clock out of sync" states. Trade-off: no revocation or view limit without a server (Keeper/Bitwarden need one); anyone with the link sees codes until expiry; default 5 min. Policy: fragment-only avoids "transmitting" authentication data, but still show in-popup prominent disclosure on first use, warn with Google's own line "Never share your verification codes with anyone," update the privacy policy and Privacy practices tab. (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3986#section-3.5, https://docs.keeper.io/en/enterprise-guide/keeper-encryption-model, https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185839?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq/)

**B. Share account as otpauth link/QR ("Move account").** Render `otpauth://totp/Issuer:account?secret=BASE32&issuer=...&algorithm&digits&period` as an on-screen QR, never saved as an image; optional `otpauth-migration://offline?data=` protobuf for Google Authenticator import. Trade-off: hands over the secret permanently; keep behind an explicit, separately-worded action, as Ente does ("QR" vs "Share"). USENIX 2023 found backup flows leaking secrets; never emit plaintext files by default. (https://github.com/google/google-authenticator/wiki/Key-Uri-Format, https://github.com/dim13/otpauth, https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity23/presentation/gilsenan)

**C. Device-to-device by QR.** Extension shows a QR, phone scans (2FAS/Signal direction); QR carries only a public key, the secret follows encrypted. No server means the two devices must exchange data another way, so this collapses to B's QR or A's pasted link. Trade-off: cleanest security, most work, and desktop `navigator.share` is unreliable (full Chrome support only from 128; Ente hides "Send" on Windows/Linux), so ship Copy link + QR, feature-detect share inside the click handler. (https://2fas.com/support/2fas-auth-browser-extension/how-to-pair-a-mobile-device-with-a-browser/, https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/share, https://github.com/ente-io/ente/blob/main/mobile/apps/auth/lib/utils/share_utils.dart)

Policy baseline for all three: authentication information is sensitive user data; HTTPS only, privacy policy, Limited Use statement on the site, disclosure of new data practices after install. (https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use/, https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/disclosure-requirements/)
